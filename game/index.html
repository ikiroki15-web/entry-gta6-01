SOLARA BAYSTREETS OF THE SUN

Rico Vale just got out of the Solara Bay county lockup with $40, a borrowed jacket and a debt to pay. The fixer Mara Quinn has work for him — if he can drive, fight and stay ahead of the Solara Bay Police long enough to finish it. Steal cars, run jobs, build your name, and don't get caught.

W A S D move / drive  ·  Mouse drag look around  ·  Shift sprint
E enter / exit car (steal parked & traffic cars)  ·  Space handbrake / jump
F punch  ·  Mouse click shoot pistol  ·  R reload  ·  1 / 2 fists / pistol
P save game  ·  Esc pause  ·  M mute  ·  C change camera

Follow the yellow marker. Gold = your next job. Blue = safe house. Red = trouble. Wanted stars rise when the police see a crime — break line of sight and lay low to cool off. Gold Sun Stashes hide around the city — grab all 8 for $50 each. Cars respawn at garages, health at the clinic, ammo at the gun shop. Your progress saves automatically after every job, or press P any time.

An original game. Solara Bay, its people and its streets are invented — no real city, no licensed cars, no borrowed characters.
